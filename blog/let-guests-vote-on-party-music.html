How to Let Your Party Guests Vote on the Music

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

One person hogging the aux is the fastest way to kill a party. The fix: let your guests vote on the music. Here's the simplest way to do it — and the app that makes it effortless.

The short answer

The easiest way to let party guests vote on the music is a group jukebox app. The host starts a session from their music library, everyone joins on their phones, and the room votes on what plays next. The most-voted song wins. Jukebox is a free iPhone app built for exactly this — and guests can join just by being nearby, no code needed.

How it works in 3 steps

  1. Host a session. Pick any Apple Music playlist or your whole library. Jukebox creates a live session with a 6-digit code and a QR code to share.
  2. Guests join and vote. Friends join by the code, by scanning the QR, or simply by being nearby — no account needed. They tap to vote on songs and can request their own.
  3. The room decides. The most-voted track plays next. Anyone can vote to skip. The music stays fair and social all night.

Why voting beats a shared playlist

A shared Spotify or Apple Music playlist lets everyone add songs, but nobody controls the order — so the vibe lurches around and one person can flood it. Voting is different: the crowd ranks the queue in real time, so the songs the room actually wants rise to the top. It feels like a democracy, not a free-for-all.

What to look for in a party voting app

  • Easy joining — a code and QR at minimum; joining "by being nearby" is even better.
  • No subscription for guests — guests should vote for free.
  • Song requests — guests should be able to add songs, not just vote on a fixed list.
  • Skip voting — a group way to move past a dud.
  • Real-time updates — vote counts should change live for everyone.

Jukebox does all five, plus time-synced lyrics, CarPlay, and a dark mode that looks great on a TV or big screen.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best way to let party guests vote on the music?

Use a group jukebox app. The host starts a session and shares a code or QR; guests join on their phones and vote on the next song. The most-voted song plays next. Jukebox is a free iPhone app that does this and even lets guests join just by being nearby — no code.

Do guests need an account or subscription to vote?

No. Only the host needs Apple Music. Guests join and vote for free — no account, no subscription.

How do guests vote on the next song?

Guests open the shared session, see the queue, and tap to vote. Each guest gets votes; the most-voted songs rise to the top and play next. Guests can also request songs and vote to skip.

Does it work with Apple Music or Spotify?

Jukebox plays through Apple Music on the host's device. Guests don't need any music service to join and vote.
